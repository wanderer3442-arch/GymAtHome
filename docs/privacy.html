Gym at Home

Privacy Policy

Effective October 29, 2026  ·  Applies to the Gym at Home mobile app

01 Most app data stays on your device

Your profile, workouts, meals, health metrics, preferences, profile photo and chat history are stored on your device. The app has no user account database or app-owned cloud sync, and Android app backups are disabled. AI prompts and weather requests are exceptions: the information described below leaves your device. Delete All Data erases Gym at Home data stored on this device; it cannot erase information already processed by an external provider. Uninstalling the Android app removes its device-local app data.

02 No advertising or analytics SDKs

The app does not include advertising or analytics SDKs. There is no account or password. To provide AI and weather features, the app sends limited information to the service providers described below; those transfers mean information does leave your device.

03 AI features

When you use an AI feature, your request is sent through our Cloudflare Worker proxy to the AI provider configured for that service (Groq or Google Gemini). A chat request can include your message, your name, profile details such as age, sex, height, weight and goals, health conditions and allergies, relevant daily meal/workout/water/sleep logs, and up to 10 recent chat messages. If you submit a CSV through chat, its contents are included. The Worker uses the connecting IP address for rate limiting and forwards the request to the configured AI provider. The app and Worker code do not save prompts or replies to an app-owned server database; chat history is saved locally on your device. The AI provider processes requests under its own terms and privacy practices, which may permit limited retention for service operation or abuse prevention. Clearing local chat or deleting local app data does not delete information already sent to a provider.

04 Weather and location (Open-Meteo)

After you grant location permission, the app can receive location updates while it is running and use them for weather. It sends your coordinates directly to Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com); updates may trigger refreshed requests. The app does not persist a location history. Open-Meteo and network providers may process request metadata, including IP address and coordinates, under their own policies. You can deny location; other app features remain available.

05 Data deletion and access

We do not have a user database from which to retrieve your locally stored profile, logs or files. The AI proxy uses your IP address for rate limiting and relays AI requests, but does not write prompts or replies to an app-owned database. External providers may independently process or retain request data under their policies. Export reports if you want a copy of locally stored data.

06 Contact

Questions about privacy or your data? Email gymathome.app@gmail.com.
